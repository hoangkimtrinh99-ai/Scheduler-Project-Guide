    AI Chatbot for Staff

    This guide explains how staff members can use the AI Chatbot assistant to manage their schedules, appointments, and daily tasks efficiently.

    Overview

    The AI Chatbot for Staff is an intelligent virtual assistant that helps staff members:

    • Quick Schedule Access: Instantly check your daily, weekly, or monthly schedule
    • Smart Appointment Management: View, create, reschedule or cancel appointments
    • Automated Leave Management: Request and create leave automatically with simple commands
    • Intelligent Assistance: Get answers to common questions about policies and procedures

    Key Features

    Natural Language Understanding

    The AI Chatbot understands conversational requests, making it easy to interact:

    • "What's on my schedule today?"
    • "Do I have any appointments tomorrow afternoon?"
    • "Reschedule my 2 PM appointment to 3 PM"
    • "Show me my availability this week"

    Real-Time Schedule Management

    View Appointments

    • Check your schedule for any date range
    • See appointment details including customer info, service type, and duration
    • Filter appointments by status (confirmed, pending, completed, cancelled)

    Modify Appointments

    • Reschedule appointments with simple commands
    • Update appointment notes or details
    • Mark appointments as completed

    Check Availability

    • Quickly find open time slots
    • See your upcoming availability windows
    • Plan your day more efficiently

    Quick Information Access

    Get instant answers to:

    • Service procedures
    • Customer history and preferences
    • Branch policies and guidelines
    • Equipment and room availability

    How to Use the AI Chatbot

    Step 1: Access the Chatbot

    • Log in to your staff portal
    • Click on the AI Chatbot icon in the bottom right corner
    • The chatbot window will open and greet you

    Step 2: Start a Conversation

    Simply type or speak your request in natural language

    Step 3: Review and Confirm

    • The chatbot will display the relevant information
    • For actions like rescheduling, you'll be asked to confirm
    • Review the details and confirm or make adjustments

    Step 4: Get Confirmation

    • Receive instant confirmation of completed actions
    • The chatbot will update your schedule automatically
    • Changes sync across all platforms (web, mobile, Outlook)

    Benefits for Staff

    Time Savings

    • Instant access to schedule information
    • Quick actions without navigating complex interfaces
    • Reduces administrative time by up to 40%

    Better Organization

    • Never miss an appointment
    • Stay informed about schedule changes
    • Proactive reminders keep you on track
    • Clear overview of your daily workload

    Enhanced Customer Service

    • Faster response times to customer requests
    • More accurate information about availability
    • Quick rescheduling without delays
    • Better preparation for appointments

    Integration with Other Systems

    The chatbot seamlessly integrates with:

    • Outlook Calendar: Syncs schedule changes automatically
    • WhatsApp Integration: Communicate with customers via WhatsApp

    Next Steps

    After setting up the AI Chatbot for Staff:

    • WhatsApp Integration - Enable customer self-service booking
    • Create Appointments - Start booking appointments

    Troubleshooting

    Cannot access chatbot: Ensure you're logged in and have staff permissions enabled.

    Chatbot not responding: Check your internet connection and try refreshing the page.

    Commands not understood: Try rephrasing your request in simpler terms or use more specific language.

    Schedule not updating: Verify you have edit permissions for your schedule and try logging out and back in.
