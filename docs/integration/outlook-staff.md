# Outlook Integration For Staff

This guide will walk you through setting up Outlook integration for individual staff members, allowing them to sync their assigned appointments with their personal Outlook calendars.

## Overview

Staff-level Outlook integration enables:

- Individual staff members to sync their assigned appointments
- Personal calendar management for each staff member
- Automatic updates when appointments are assigned or modified

## Setup Outlook Integration For Staff

### Step 1: Navigate to Staff Management
- In the left-hand side menu, navigate to **Staffs** → **Staffs**
- Click ![Edit](../image/Edit%20pencil.png) **Edit** on the staff member you want to set up Outlook integration for

![Staff Outlook 1](../image/Staff%20outlook%201.png)

### Step 2: Send Authentication URL
- In the staff member's profile, locate the **Outlook Integration** section
- Click **Send Authentication URL**

![Staff Outlook 2](../image/Staff%20outlook%202.png)

### Step 3: Staff Authentication Process
- An authentication URL will be sent to the staff member's email address
- The staff member needs to:
    - Check their email for the authentication URL
    - Click on the URL to open the authentication page
    - Sign in with their Microsoft account
    - Grant necessary permissions for calendar access
    - Confirm the integration request
## Benefits

### Personal Calendar Management
- Staff can view their appointments in their personal calendar
- Integration with existing Outlook workflow
- Reduced need to check multiple systems

### Real-time Updates
- Automatic sync of appointment assignments
- Immediate updates in personal Outlook calendar
- Consistent information across systems

### Individual Control
- Each staff member controls their own integration
- Personal calendar privacy maintained
- Flexible integration management

## Troubleshooting

**Cannot see Staffs menu**: Ensure you have admin permissions to access staff management.

**Authentication URL not sent**: Check staff member's email address and try again.

**Staff cannot access URL**: Ensure staff member has access to their email and Microsoft account.

**Sync not working**: Verify staff member completed authentication and try reconnecting.

**Missing appointments**: Check that appointments are properly assigned to the staff member.

**Permission errors**: Ensure staff member's Microsoft account has appropriate calendar access permissions.
