# WhatsApp Integration for Customers

This guide explains how customers can use WhatsApp to book appointments, reschedule, and manage their bookings automatically through conversational messaging.

## Overview

WhatsApp Integration enables customers to manage their appointments through the world's most popular messaging app. This feature provides:

- **Automated Booking**: Book appointments through natural conversation
- **Instant Rescheduling**: Push back or change appointment times easily
- **Quick Cancellations**: Cancel appointments with simple commands
- **24/7 Availability**: Self-service booking anytime, anywhere
- **Real-Time Confirmations**: Instant booking confirmations and reminders

## Key Features

### Conversational Booking

Customers can book appointments using natural language:

- "I'd like to book a consultation for next Monday"
- "Do you have any availability tomorrow afternoon?"
- "Book me for a 2 PM slot this Friday"
- "What times are available this week?"

### Smart Rescheduling

Easy appointment modifications:

- "Can I move my appointment to next week?"
- "Reschedule my Thursday appointment to Friday same time"
- "Push back my appointment by 2 hours"
- "What other times are available?"

### Simple Cancellations

Quick cancellations:

- "Cancel my appointment for tomorrow"
- "I need to cancel my Friday booking"
- "Remove my 3 PM appointment"

## How Customers Use WhatsApp Integration

Customers can connect in several ways:

- Scan the QR code on your website
- Save your business WhatsApp number

### Integration with Scheduler

The WhatsApp connects directly to your scheduler system:

- Real-time availability checking
- Automatic staff assignment based on workload
- Resource (room/equipment) allocation
- Conflict prevention
- Calendar synchronization
