# Setup Staff / Counsellors

This guide will walk you through adding staff members to your scheduler system.

## Overview

Staff members are the people who provide services to your customers. Each staff member can be assigned to specific services, branches, and can have specializations and skills associated with them.

## Setup Staff / Counsellors

### Step 1: Navigate to Staff Management
- In the left-hand side menu, navigate to **Staffs** → **Staffs**
- Click **Create Staff**

![Create Staff 1](../image/create%20Staff%201.png)

### Step 2: Input Basic Staff Information

- **Name**: Enter the staff member's full name
- **Email**: Enter the staff member's email address (used for notifications and login)
- **Branch**: Select which branch the staff member works at

### Step 3: Input Specializations and Skills (Optional)
- **Select Specializations**: Choose from previously created specializations
- **Select Skills**: Choose from previously created skills

### Step 4: Service Availability
- **Select Services**: Choose which services the staff member can be booked for or leave as "All Services"

![Create Staff 2](../image/create%20staff%202.png)

### Step 5: Save Staff Member
- Click **Create Staff** to add the staff member

## Troubleshooting

**Cannot see Staffs menu**: Ensure you have admin permissions to access staff management.

**Staff not saving**: Verify all required fields are filled and try again.

**No specializations/skills available**: Complete the [Specialization & Skills setup](specialization-skills.md) first (optional).

**No services available**: Complete the [Service setup](service.md) first.

**Email already in use**: Each staff member must have a unique email address.

**Branch not available**: Ensure the branch exists and is active.
