# Setup Staff Schedules / Rosters

This guide will walk you through creating staff schedules and rosters in the scheduler system.

## Overview

Staff schedules define when staff members are available to provide services. These schedules can be created for individual staff or multiple staff members at once, and can be repeated over multiple weeks.

## Setup Staff Schedules / Rosters

### Step 1: Navigate to Staff Schedules
- In the left-hand side menu, navigate to **Staffs** → **Schedules**
- Click **Add Schedule**

![Create Staff Schedule 1](../image/Create%20staff%20sche%201.png)

### Step 2: Select Staff Members
Select the staff members you want to create schedules for. You can select multiple staff members at once

### Step 3: Schedule Details
- **Select Branch**: Choose the branch for this schedule
- **Select Shift Template**: For each day of the week, select the appropriate shift template (use the shift templates created in previous setup)

### Step 4: Copy Schedule Pattern
- **Set First Day**: Set the schedule for Monday (or first day of your work week)
- **Copy to All Days**: Click this button to apply the same schedule to all days of the week
- **Toggle Repeat**: Enable this to set how many weeks the schedule should be repeated

![Create Staff Schedule 2](../image/Create%20Staff%20sche%202.png)

### Step 5: Save Schedule
Click **Add Schedule** to create the schedule

## Troubleshooting

**Cannot see Schedules menu**: Ensure you have admin permissions to access staff scheduling.

**No staff members available**: Complete the [Staff setup](staff.md) first.

**No shift templates available**: Complete the [Staff Shift Template setup](staff-shift-template.md) first.

**Schedule not saving**: Verify all required fields are filled and try again.

**Conflicting schedules**: Ensure staff members don't have overlapping schedules for the same time period.

**Template mismatch**: Ensure shift templates are compatible with branch operating hours.
